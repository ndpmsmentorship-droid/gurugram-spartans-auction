01What the index does

The Shanti Devi Players Index (SPI) gives every approved player one score from 0 to 1000, built from their batting, bowling and fielding records. Each player then gets two ranks: an overall rank across the whole pool and an age-bracket rank against players of their own age bracket. Categories follow from the bracket rank. Decided

03Small samples

A record is a small sample when it is below 30% of the pool median: fewer than 30% of the median player’s matches for batting, or 30% of the median player’s wickets for bowling. A small-sample skill gets a standard low score of 10 out of 100 instead of its percentile. Proposed

The cut-off uses the median, not the average, because a few very long careers pull the average up. It moves with each season’s pool, so it never needs a fixed number. On the league’s 1,000-player database (3 Nov 2026):

30% of the median catches the thin records (about 1 player in 11 for batting) without marking down players with long careers. Bowling flags more players because many batters rarely bowl; for them the low Bowl score changes nothing, as the SPI counts their stronger skill most.

Players with no record are Unrated: they go into category B, and the panel may move them after a trial or other evidence. Proposed

04Batting, bowling and fielding scores

Each figure becomes a percentile within the whole pool (0 = lowest, 100 = highest), so runs, rates and averages can be added fairly. For economy and bowling average, lower is better, so the order is reversed. Proposed

ScoreBuilt fromWeights
Batting BatBatting average · strike rate · boundary %40% · 30% · 30%
Bowling BowlWickets per match · economy (lower better) · bowling average (lower better)40% · 30% · 30%
Fielding FldCatches per match, plus stumpings for keepers100%

Boundary % is the share of balls faced that the player hit for four or six: (4s + 6s) ÷ balls faced × 100. Balls faced come from runs ÷ strike rate × 100. Proposed

05Role: batter, bowler or all-rounder

Role comes from the two main scores, so players who are strong at both (the overlap) are recognised as all-rounders. Proposed

RoleRule
All-rounderBat ≥ 60 and Bowl ≥ 60: in the top 40% of the pool for both
BatterNot an all-rounder, and Bat ≥ Bowl
BowlerNot an all-rounder, and Bowl > Bat
Wicket-keeperA flag added to the role when the player keeps (registration and records)

When the two scores are within 2 points, the role the player declared on registration decides.

06The SPI score

One formula covers every role. The stronger skill counts most, the second skill adds to it, and fielding adds a little. That way a genuine all-rounder ranks well without beating a clearly better specialist. Proposed

SPI = 10 × ( 0.70 × max(Bat, Bowl) + 0.20 × min(Bat, Bowl) + 0.10 × Fld )

The score runs from 0 to 1000. Ties are split by career matches (more first), then by registration date (earlier first).

08Categories

Under 35

Every player under 35 is A+, whatever their score. They still get an overall rank and a bracket rank. Decided

35–39

The top N by bracket rank are A; the rest are B. Decided

40+

Same as 35–39, ranked only against other 40+ players. Decided

How many make A

“Enough” means enough A players for 14 teams to hold up to 6 each, with some choice left. The A places are shared between the 35–39 and 40+ brackets in proportion to how many players each bracket has. Proposed

A places = 14 teams × 6 A per squad × 1.25 = 105 bracket A = 105 × (players in bracket ÷ players in both) never more than half the bracket

Legends

A player is placed in the Legend category, instead of A or B, if they are 60 or older on the cut-off date, or their career batting average is below 10 and their career strike rate is below 100. This test uses the raw career figures, before any adjustment. The Board may also name any player a Legend, or make an exception, on a reasonable basis that it records. Legends keep both SPI ranks and are left out of the A count above. Decided

Balance by role

The A seats in each bracket are shared out in proportion to that bracket’s mix of batters, bowlers and all-rounders. Each share is filled from the top of its role list, and any seats left over go to the next-best SPI. This keeps A from filling up with batters alone. Proposed

10Governance

  1. G1A grading panel of at least three people runs the index and signs off the categories. Proposed
  2. G2The panel may move a player by one category, with written reasons, when the record clearly misleads, for example after a long injury or with a thin, very old record. Proposed
  3. G3A player may ask once for a review before the pool is published. The panel’s decision after review is final. Proposed
  4. G4Scores, ranks and categories are frozen when the auction pool is published. Proposed
  5. G5The weights and thresholds in this document are reviewed after each season. Proposed
