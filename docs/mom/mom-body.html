Shanti Devi Legends League (SDLL)

Minutes of Meeting

Season 2 – Captains’ Meet
Meeting TitleSDLL Season 2 – Captains’ Meet
Date30 October
Time7:00 PM
Venue / PlatformVivanta by Taj, Dwarka
AttendeesSDLL Management Team, Team Owners, Captains, and other stakeholders.

Section 1Objective of the Meeting

To discuss the key policies and operating framework for SDLL Season 2, including player registration and onboarding, tournament scheduling, team composition, player eligibility and retention, Legends policy, financial distribution, and team-management controls.

Section 2Agenda and Discussion Points

A. Player Registration and Onboarding

1. Player Registration Process

  • The end-to-end player registration workflow for SDLL Season 2 was discussed, including registration steps, eligibility requirements, and mandatory information to be captured.
  • A standardized process for registration confirmation is to be established.

2. Player Onboarding Scenario

  • The onboarding journey from registration through shortlisting and final selection was discussed.
  • A structured communication process is to be defined for registered, shortlisted, selected, and non-selected players, along with clear timelines for each stage.

3. Registration Fee Revision

  • The proposal to revise the player registration fee from ₹3,000 to ₹4,000 was discussed.
  • The impact of the proposed revision on player participation and tournament operations is to be evaluated before the applicable fee is confirmed.

4. Player Profile Scrutiny

  • Player profiles will be scrutinized by the SDLL Management Team, with reference to the applicable SLL Season 1 terms and conditions.
  • The scrutiny process will cover the accuracy of player details, cricketing background, category, and eligibility.
  • The earlier registration requirements include a mandatory player photograph and father’s name; the Instagram handle is optional.
  • A process is to be defined for addressing incomplete, incorrect, or misleading profile information.

5. Player Non-Selection Terms and Confirmations

  • The terms applicable to players who register but are not selected were discussed.
  • The refund position, if any, for the registration fee is to be clarified and communicated before registration.
  • Players should acknowledge the applicable non-selection terms and conditions as part of registration.

6. Editable Email IDs in the Player Portal

  • The possibility of allowing players to edit their registered email IDs through the portal was discussed.
  • Any email change should be subject to verification and authentication to maintain accurate communication and registration records.

7. Additional Player Profile Requirements

  • The proposal to collect LinkedIn profile details from players who own businesses or have relevant professional or business interests was discussed.
  • The applicability, additional information to be collected, and verification criteria are to be defined.

8. Player Accountability Undertaking

  • A formal undertaking for players receiving monetary compensation for participating in the tournament was discussed.
  • The undertaking is intended to set out player commitments relating to availability, participation, and agreed financial terms.
  • The consequences of non-compliance are to be clearly defined in the undertaking.

B. Tournament Schedule and Dates

1. Tournament Dates

  • The proposed SDLL Season 2 tournament dates were discussed, taking into consideration player availability, ground availability, and potential scheduling conflicts.
  • The final tournament calendar is to be confirmed and communicated to participating teams.

2. Semi-Final and Final Dates

  • The proposed dates for the semi-finals and final were reviewed.
  • The schedule should be assessed against other ongoing and upcoming cricket tournaments to minimize clashes that may affect player availability, team strength, or audience participation.

C. Player Retention and Eligibility Policies

1. Player Retention Policy

  • Retention eligibility was discussed with reference to participation in SLL Season 1.
  • As recorded in the earlier policy discussion, only players who participated in SLL Season 1 are eligible for consideration for retention.
  • The maximum retention per team is two players: one from Category A and one from Category B / Legends.
  • The final retention conditions, process, and applicable deadlines are to be confirmed and communicated.

2. Team and Squad Combinations

  • The squad-size limits and Playing 12 format were discussed, along with age-based eligibility and Category A / Category B classification.

  • A celebrity personality may be invited to play by each team owner.

3. Legends Eligibility Criteria and Policy

  • The previously documented Legends policy provides for a maximum of two Legends per team.
  • The recorded performance benchmarks for Legends are: batting average below 10 and strike rate below 100.
  • At least one Legend must play in each match; otherwise, a penalty of 25 runs applies.
  • A Legend is required to bat in the top four.
  • The eligibility, classification, and participation criteria are to be applied consistently across teams.

4. Run Allotment for Legends

  • The proposed special run-allotment / scoring provisions for Legends were discussed in relation to match balance and competitive fairness.
  • Decided: for the duration of a Legend’s innings, 1 run is counted as 2 runs, 4 runs as 6 runs, and 6 runs as 8 runs.
  • The final rule should be communicated to all teams before the tournament.

D. Financial Policies and Prize Distribution

1. Chit Amount and Potential Clashes

  • The proposed chit / player amount in the event of a financial clash between teams was discussed.
  • The applicable amount and process are to be clarified and documented.

2. Winning Amount Distribution Between Players and Owners

  • The distribution of the winning amount between team owners and players was discussed.
  • A transparent allocation framework is to be established, including the responsibilities of team owners and SDLL Management in communicating and implementing the agreed distribution.

3. Amount Brackets Proposed by Team Owners

  • Team owners will determine their proposed amount brackets and submit them to the SDLL Management Team.
  • The submission deadline and process for reviewing and consolidating the proposals are to be communicated.

4. Secret Polling for Amount Distribution

  • A confidential poll was discussed as a mechanism to identify the preferred prize-money distribution model.
  • The proposed 60:40 distribution model was reviewed.
  • The exact interpretation of the 60:40 ratio—including the share allocated to players and the share allocated to owners—must be explicitly confirmed before implementation.
  • The SDLL Management Team will facilitate the polling process and communicate the outcome once approved.

E. Team Management and Administrative Controls

1. Team Composition Visibility for Administrators

  • The visibility of team composition and squad details within the administrative portal was discussed.
  • Access permissions are to be defined for the SDLL Management Team and authorized administrators.
  • Appropriate confidentiality and access controls should be implemented to prevent unauthorized access to team information.

Section 3Decisions and Resolutions

The matters captured in these minutes reflect the discussion points and the relevant policy references available at the time of preparation. Items requiring confirmation—including fee finalization, refund terms, tournament dates, financial brackets, and the 60:40 allocation—should be formally closed by the SDLL Management Team before implementation.
